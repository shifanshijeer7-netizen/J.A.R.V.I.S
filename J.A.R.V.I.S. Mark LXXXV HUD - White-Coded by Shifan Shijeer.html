WHAT WE FIXED IN THIS CODE FOR YOUR APK:

Standard Android WebViews do NOT support window.speechSynthesis or webkitSpeechRecognition. This edition bypasses both! It records your voice using a Direct 16kHz PCM-to-WAV AudioContext Recorder and plays JARVIS's voice as a standard RIFF WAVE (audio/wav) stream through HTML5 <audio> + Web Audio API.

IF YOUR APK CONVERTER STILL BLOCKS THE MICROPHONE:

When converting HTML to APK (in apps like Website2APK, WebIntoApp, or HopWeb), make sure these 3 settings are enabled in your APK builder:

  • Enable Android Permission: RECORD_AUDIO & MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS
  • Enable Android Permission: CAMERA & INTERNET
  • Enable WebView option: Allow WebRTC / Audio & Video Capture (and go to Android Settings → Apps → Your JARVIS App → Permissions → Allow Microphone & Camera).
